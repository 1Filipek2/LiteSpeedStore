Detailed Description

Append-only write-ahead log with a SHA-256 hash chain for tamper evidence. Entry layout: CRC32 | seq | timestamp | epoch | key_len | value_len | type | key | value | chain_hash, where chain_hash = SHA256(prev_hash | seq..value).
