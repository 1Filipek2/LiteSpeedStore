Outcome of walking the log chain during recovery or verification.

Enumerator
Ok 

The whole chain verified cleanly.

TruncatedTail 

A partial/torn write at the end was discarded (crash recovery).

Malformed 

A field exceeds the policy cap: a foreign or stale file, not tamper evidence.

Tampered 

An entry was modified, reordered, or deleted in place.
